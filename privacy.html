Privacy policy

Amane Shell Calendar asks Google for read-only access to your calendar list and calendar events. It uses them only to show your events in the shell and to send reminders on your own computer.

The events and the sign-in token are stored only on that computer, in files only your user can read. They are not sent to any other server, sold, or shared with anyone.

You can remove the access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
